Market & token risks +

Gold-related token values may change. Different tokens can trade at different prices and can carry issuer, redemption and liquidity risks.

Trading & network costs +

Blockchain fees, spreads, exchange costs, slippage and unfavorable price movements may reduce asset value.

Smart-contract & system risks +

Contracts and automated systems may contain bugs, vulnerabilities, execution errors or interruptions.

Approvals & wallet security +

Token allowances and wallet permissions involve risk. Review, limit and revoke permissions where appropriate and protect private keys.
